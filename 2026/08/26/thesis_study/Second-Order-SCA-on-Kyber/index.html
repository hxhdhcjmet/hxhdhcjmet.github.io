Second-Order Side-Channel Attacks on Kyber: Targeting the Masked Hash Function

Author: WANG Ya-Qi; HUANG Fan; DUAN Xiao-Lin; HU Hong-Gang;

Journal: ,

Journal Tags:

Local Link: Ya-Qi 等 - Second-Order Side-Channel Attacks on Kyber Targeting the Masked Hash Function.pdf

URL:

Abstract: Recently, several PC oracle based side-channel attacks have been proposed against Kyber. However, most of them focus on unprotected implementations and masking is considered as a countermeasure. In this study, we extend PC oracle based side-channel attacks to the second-order scenario and successfully conduct key-recovery attacks on the first-order masked Kyber. Firstly, we analyze the potential joint information leakage. Inspired by the binary PC oracle based attack proposed by Qin et al. at Asiacrypt 2021, we identify the 1-bit leakage scenario in the masked Keccak implementation. Moreover, we modify the ciphertexts construction described by Tanaka et al. at CHES 2023, extending the leakage scenario from 1-bit to 32-bit. With the assistance of TVLA, we validate these leakages through experiments. Secondly, for these two scenarios, we construct a binary PC oracle based on t-test and a multiple-valued PC oracle based on neural networks. Furthermore, we conduct practical side-channel attacks on masked Kyber by utilizing our oracles, with the implementation running on an ARM Cortex-M4 microcontroller. The demonstrated attacks require a minimum of 15 788 and 648 traces to fully recover the key of Kyber768 in the 1-bit leakage scenario and the 32-bit leakage scenario, respectively. Our analysis may also be extended to attack other post-quantum schemes that use the same masked hash function. Finally, we apply the shuffling strategy to the first-order masked implementation of the Kyber and perform leakage tests. Experimental results show that the combination strategy of shuffling and masking can effectively resist our proposed attacks.

Contributions

Vulnerability under Second-Order Attacks

Extend PC Oracle Based Attacks to Second-Order Scenario

  • first order masked下，mmm被分为两份share，分别为m0,m1m^0,m^1m0,m1,满足

    m=m0⊕m1m = m^0 \oplus m^1 m=m0⊕m1

其中m0,m1m^0,m^1m0,m1满足:

  • 当m=m0m=m_0m=m0​时，m0m^0m0与m1m^1m1的第一字节相同，即m0[0]=m1[0]m^0[0] = m^1[0]m0[0]=m1[0]，且m0[0]⊕m1[0]=0x00m^0[0] \oplus m^1[0] = \text{0x00}m0[0]⊕m1[0]=0x00。

  • 当m=m1m= m_1m=m1​时，m0m^0m0与m1m^1m1的第一字节仅一个比特不同，即m0[0]⊕m1[0]=0x01m^0[0] \oplus m^1[0] = \text{0x01}m0[0]⊕m1[0]=0x01。

  • m0、m1m^0、m^1m0、m1的剩余字节均相同，即m0[i]=m1[i],i∈{1,2,⋯31}m^0[i] = m^1[i] ,i \in \{1,2,\cdots 31\}m0[i]=m1[i],i∈{1,2,⋯31}。

    注，以上场景是binary PC oracle 扩展到first order masked上的情况，mmm被构造成第一字节第一比特要么为0，要么为1的情况，即m0=(0x00,0x00,...,0x00)m_0 = (\text{0x00,0x00,...,0x00})m0​=(0x00,0x00,...,0x00),m1=(0x01,0x00,…,0x00)m_1 = \text{(0x01,0x00,…,0x00)}m1​=(0x01,0x00,…,0x00)。

  • 后续如果m0m^0m0,m1m^1m1有过相同的操作，根据能量泄露模型，攻击者可以分别检出汉明重量泄露。使用SCA泄露m0、m1m^0、m^1m0、m1的第一字节的汉明重量，分别记为HW(m0[0])、HW(m1[0])HW(m^0[0])、HW(m^1[0])HW(m0[0])、HW(m1[0])，有以下两种情况：

    • 如果m=m0m= m_0m=m0​(根据上文有此时m0m^0m0与m1m^1m1的第一字节相同)，则∣HW(m0[0])−HW(m1[0])∣=0|HW(m^0[0]) - HW(m^1[0])| = 0∣HW(m0[0])−HW(m1[0])∣=0，相应的能量泄露几乎相同。

    • 如果m=m1m=m_1m=m1​(同上，此时m0m^0m0与m1m^1m1仅第一字节的第一比特不同)，则∣HW(m0[0])−HW(m1[0])∣=1|HW(m^0[0]) - HW(m^1[0])| = 1∣HW(m0[0])−HW(m1[0])∣=1，相应的能量泄露仅1比特不同。

攻击者若能区分以上两种情况，利用能量消耗的差异就能构造出一个PC  Oracle，那么这个first-order masking implementation就能被SCA攻破。

可以发现，这类攻击关键都是要构造出对应的Oracle。

Vulnerability in the Masked Keccak Implementation

  • Kyber中使用的哈希函数SHA-3是Keccak算法(海绵结构哈希函数，不同于以前的MD结构)，分为吸收和挤压两个阶段，内部包含一个5×5×85\times 5 \times 85×5×8字节的矩阵ststst，该矩阵首先初始化为全0，然后与输入逐字节异或，填充后作为Keccak-f置换的输入，该置换操作将执行NroundN_{round}Nround​轮迭代。

  • 在first-order masked hash function中，内部状态ststst被分为两份share：st0,st1st^0,st^1st0,st1并分别为初始化为0。这两份share会分别与输入buf0,buf1\text {buf}^0,\text{buf}^1buf0,buf1做逐字节异或操作。具体算法可见下图:

    这里的两份share分别就是m0m^0m0和m1m^1m1，作为masked Keccak function的输入。在吸收阶段的逐字节异或操作中，这两份share会泄露对应的能量信息。由于mmm仅取两种可能的值（即m=m0m = m_0m=m0​或m=m1m= m_1m=m1​），攻击者可以利用两个share各自的第一字节的异或操作的泄露获取丰富的信息。即联合利用m0[0]m^0[0]m0[0]、m1[0]m^1[0]m1[0]来区分m=m0m=m_0m=m0​ or m=m1m=m_1m=m1​。因为攻击者每次观察可以获取一比特信息，当下场景称为1-bit leakage scenario.

Countermeasures

  • Shuffling：结合masking和Shuffling，即使得在masked hash function 中的XOR操作的顺序被打乱，这样将使得不同share对相同字节的操作难以对齐。也就是说，shuffling操作打乱leakage points，使得攻击者更难提取出有意义的信息。masking + shuffling保护下的t-test结果如下图：

    可以看到，t-test value均小于阈值。

  • Higher-order Masking：本实验的攻击方法未必对更高阶的掩码有效，不过更高阶的掩码也意味着更高的开销。

  • Key Refreshment：在攻击成功所需的查询次数之前就将私钥刷新，这样攻击者也无法及时恢复完整私钥。

📜 Conclusion

  • 本论文将PC oracle based SCA key recovery对Kyber的攻击扩展到一阶掩码的攻击上。论文展开了对masked hash function泄露的分析，发现masked  hash function一次至多可能泄露32bit明文信息（取决于密文的构建）。定义并验证了1-bit leakage  scenario和32-bit leakage scenario。

  • 1-bit leakage scenario中使用基于t-test的binary oracle，每次查询oracle的平均traces数为8.9，正确率为99.95%99.95\%99.95%。32-bit leakage scenario中oracle构建综合了neural network 和 majority-voting，得到平均每次查询所需traces为9。
