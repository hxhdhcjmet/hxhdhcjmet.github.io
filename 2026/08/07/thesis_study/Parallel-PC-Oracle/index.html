(2023-03-06) Pushing the Limits of Generic Side-Channel Attacks on LWE-based KEMs - Parallel PC Oracle Attacks on Kyber KEM and Beyond
Author: Gokulnath Rajendran; Prasanna Ravi; Jan-Pieter D’Anvers; Shivam Bhasin; Anupam Chattopadhyay;
Journal: IACR Transactions on Cryptographic Hardware and Embedded Systems, 418-446, 2023.
Journal Tags:
Local Link: Rajendran 等 - 2023 - Pushing the Limits of Generic Side-Channel Attacks on LWE-based KEMs - Parallel PC Oracle Attacks on.pdf
DOI: 10.46586/tches.v2023.i2.418-446
Abstract: In this work, we propose generic and novel adaptations to the binary Plaintext-Checking (PC) oracle based side-channel attacks for Kyber KEM. These attacks operate in a chosen-ciphertext setting, and are fairly generic and easy to mount on a given target, as the attacker requires very minimal information about the target device. However, these attacks have an inherent disadvantage of requiring a few thousand traces to perform full key recovery. This is due to the fact that these attacks typically work by recovering a single bit of information about the secret key per query/trace. In this respect, we propose novel parallel PC oracle based side-channel attacks, which are capable of recovering a generic P number of bits of information about the secret key in a single query/trace. We propose novel techniques to build chosen-ciphertexts so as to eﬃciently realize a parallel PC oracle for Kyber KEM. We also build a multi-class classiﬁer, which is capable of realizing a practical side-channel based parallel PC oracle with very high success rate. We experimentally validated the proposed attacks (upto P = 10) on the fastest implementation of unprotected Kyber KEM in the pqm4 library. Our experiments yielded improvements in the range of 2.89× and 7.65× in the number of queries, compared to state-of-the-art binary PC oracle attacks, while arbitrarily higher improvements are possible for a motivated attacker, given the generic nature of the proposed attacks. We further conduct a thorough study on applicability to diﬀerent scenarios, based on the presence/absence of a clone device, and also partial key recovery. Finally, we also show that the proposed attacks are able to achieve the lowest number of queries for key recovery, even for implementations protected with low-cost countermeasures such as shuﬄing. Our work therefore, concretely demonstrates the power of PC oracle attacks on Kyber KEM, thereby stressing the need for concrete countermeasures such as masking for Kyber and other lattice-based KEMs.

📜 Research Core

Contributions

  • 提出一种通用、新颖的使用SCA辅助的基于PC Oracle的攻击,即P路并行PC Oracle攻击，每次可以同时恢复Pbit信息，对比目前最先进的PC Oracle攻击每次只能恢复1bit信息。
  • 发现现有基于二进制（一般的一次恢复一位的)PC Oracle的最佳选择密文查询对于本文所提出的并行PC Oracle并不一定是最优的，因此本文单独提出更优的构建方法(即Binary Decision Trees,BDTs)来确定查询次数的下界。
  • 使用二进制PC Oraccle的分类器来实现P 路PC Oracle的总共 2P2^P2P 种类的分类器，且在 P=10P = 10P=10 时达到分类的完美准确率(更大 PPP 只存在于理论中，实际很难评估 PPP 的上界)。
  • 实验验证基于pqm4的攻击效果是现有最先进二进制PC Oracle 的2.89倍和7.65倍。
  • 分析了两种情况(1.有无克隆设备2.敌手离线计算能力)下的攻击情况。
  • 实际验证了本文提出的攻击在低开销策略保护(如混洗)下的应用效果，显示为现有侧信道辅助的选择密文攻击的最好效果(需要的traces数最小)。

Prior Side-Channel Attacks and Motivation

TO_Indep
  • PC Oracle-based SCA: 选择密文使得解密结果严格限制为只有 m=0m=0m=0 和 m=1m=1m=1 两种，利用时间侧信道攻击(即ECC_Decode(0)与ECC_Decode(1)的时间不同)实现PC Oracle, m=0m=0m=0 和 m=1m=1m=1 的1bit不同都会对re-encryption过程的计算造成巨大影响(即一条trace即可区分).。
  • DF Oracle-based SCA:攻击者能探测出解密失败(m=m_valid or m = m_invalid),这里探测方法如Guo等人利用密文比较的时间差异(re-encryption后的c == c’比较操作，是否相同的比较时间有差异。实际上不只可以利用比较操作，整个re-encryption过程都可利用)来构建DF Oracle。
TO Dep
  • Encoding/Decoding Operation:利用编码和解码操作操作泄露的信息，使得只利用极少的trace就能恢复完整信息m.但是这种方法有很大局限性:只利用单个时钟周期或非常少的采样点来恢复信息的一比特,因此对噪声(SNR,信噪比)非常敏感，同时也不清楚类似的泄露是否能在更复杂的设备，如重度平行、深流水线、固有抖动等特征下的设备。
  • NTT Operation:利用Kyber中的NTT操作，同样只需要少量trace。但是同样面临如需要复杂设置和强大侧信道攻击能力、难以实施、在复杂设备平台上运行效果未知等缺点。

💡 Innovations

Multi-Class Classfication

  • 如果能对 m∈[0,2P−1]m \in [0,2^{P-1}]m∈[0,2P−1] 中任意一对 mmm 做区分，那么就有可能单独辨别出所有的 2P2^P2P 种的 mmm 。

  • 基于前面Binary Classifination的过程，这里Multi-Class Classfication也分为两步:

    • Pre-Procress:对所有 m∈[0,2P−1]m \in [0,2^{P-1}]m∈[0,2P−1] ,每种收集 TTT 条re-encryption的trace(总共要收集 2P⋅T2^P \cdot T2P⋅T 条trace )。
    • Classification:采用两两比较的方式(pairwise binary classification)确定某条实验收集到的trace到底属于哪一类。

Partial Key Recovery

  • 本论文提出的P-way Parallel PC Oracle可以只用来恢复 ststst 的部分比特，剩下位的比特保留下来用离线方式计算出来，权衡在线查询的次数和离线计算的难度。

🧩 Shortcomings/FutureWork

  • 完美分类的 PPP 的上界尚不清楚，论文指出如果该上界存在，那么将很难通过实验来确定，因为它实际取决于很多因素，比如目标设备、目标设备实现、信噪比等，因此目前对 PPP 的选择确定只能基于经验，具体确定作为未来工作。

Extension to Lightly Protected Implementations

  • masking技术用于LWE/LWR-based KEMs最高能造成3倍的时间开销，因此低开销的抵御已知攻击的保护手段备受关注，一种方式就是shuffing。它使得攻击者依然能恢复完整的信息，但是这些信息的比特顺序是乱序的，因而提供保护并去除了Full-Decryption Oracle结构。
  • Ravi提出一种选择密文攻击，可以每次查询恢复1bit信息(等同于binry PC Oracle)。

📜 Conclusion

  • 本论文提出一种新颖的基于SCA的并行PC Oracle,可以一次性根据一条trace恢复(任意) PPP bit信息，在基于pqm4的实现上实验证明该方法较目前最先进(之前论文中的mismatch attack)binary PC Oracle在查询次数上有了很大改进。
  • 本论文提出的方法(P-way Parallel PC Oracle)在低成本保护(shuffling)的设备上同样达到了恢复密钥所需的最低查询次数，然而该方法能被掩码技术有效阻止，进一步证明了掩码技术在基于格的密码算法上的必要性。
