(2020) Magnifying Side-Channel Leakage of Lattice-Based Cryptosystems With Chosen Ciphertexts: The Case Study of Kyber
Author: Zhuang Xu; Owen Pemberton; Sujoy Sinha Roy; David Oswald; Wang Yao; Zhiming Zheng;
Journal: , 2020.
Journal Tags:
Local Link: Xu 等 - 2020 - Magnifying Side-Channel Leakage of Lattice-Based Cryptosystems With Chosen Ciphertexts The Case Stu.pdf
URL: https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/912
Abstract: Lattice-based cryptography, as an active branch of post-quantum cryptography (PQC), has drawn great attention from side-channel analysis researchers in recent years. Despite the various side-channel targets examined in previous studies, detail on revealing the secret-dependent information efficiently is less studied. In this paper, we propose adaptive EM side-channel attacks with carefully constructed ciphertexts on Kyber, which is a finalist of NIST PQC standardization project. We demonstrate that specially chosen ciphertexts allow an adversary to modulate the leakage of a target device and enable full key extraction with a small number of traces through simple power analysis. Compared to prior research, our techniques require fewer traces and avoid building complex templates. We practically evaluate our methods using both a reference implementation and the ARM-specific implementation in pqm4 library. For the reference implementation, we target the leakage of the output of the inverse NTT computation and recover the full key with only four traces. For the pqm4 implementation, we develop a message-recovery attack that leads to extraction of the full secret key with between eight and 960 traces, depending on the compiler optimization level. We discuss the relevance of our findings to other lattice-based schemes and explore potential countermeasures.

🔁 Research Content

📜 Conclusion

  • 攻击者选定特殊的操作阶段，构造特定的密文，利用解密时所选定的操作阶段泄露的侧信道信息，实现恢复完整信息和私钥。
  • 简单的C实现Kyber可以利用解密时Inverse NTT操作泄露的信息，利用少量的trace以极高的概率( ∼100%\sim 100\%∼100% )恢复出完整私钥；在汇编优化的pqm4实现的Kyber，虽然无法直接利用SCA恢复出私钥，但可以以极高概率恢复出完整明文，再利用明文信息与私钥之间的关系恢复出完整私钥。

Countermeasures

  • 私钥不重用/较少私钥的重复使用，这样即使恢复出完整私钥也无法用于下一次攻击。
  • 利用masking-based countermeasure或shuffling技术(但这些技术会使得密码算法整体运行开销大大增加)。
  • 利用检测机制，当检测到恶意密文时直接丢弃而不直接进行解密操作。因为特殊构建的密文相较于正常密文在结构上有很明显的差异，比如特殊构建的密文会有很多特定位总是0。同时，正常的真实密文本质上应当是LWE上的采样样本，与随机采样不可区分，因此可以通过测量收到的密文的熵来决定丢弃某些恶意密文(比如那些墒值较低的密文)。

🤔 Personal Summary

🙋♀️ Key Records

  • 这篇论文本质是通过构造FD Oracle来实现利用极少数trace即可恢复完整密钥的攻击方式。因为这里最终是需要恢复出完整的明文信息。
  • 本论文中利用的泄漏点都是逐比特/系数操作，即独立性很强的操作，每一位比特/系数只利用该操作处理到它这一位的那一段trace泄露，即可恢复出有关信息。与MV-PC oracle那篇论文对比(以及那篇论文中提到的本篇的FD Oracle的缺点)，本方法只需要（也只能）利用某一时钟周期或少数采样点的数据，因此对噪声非尝敏感，且在更高度优化的如高度平行化、深度流水、固有抖动、深度优化等场景下使用效果大打折扣。
  • 该论文中第一种攻击方法之所以可以实现少数几条trace就能恢复完整私钥，是因为一条trace的某一小部分专门反应了 sss / mmm 的一位比特(系数)，具有很高的独立性。这里，如何确定trace中的某一部分就是私钥/明文的这一位(精准定位双射sksksk/mmm ↔\leftrightarrow↔ precise trace samples)、如何精准的捕获poly_frommsg()、fqmul()这种很短的操作(相较于前一篇整个的re-encryption过程)是需要解决的问题。
